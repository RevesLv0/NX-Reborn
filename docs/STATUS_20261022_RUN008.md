## New result

[Temperature and power boundaries](../analysis/architecture/THERMAL_POWER_RUN008.md)
connect the app's Sensor/CPU/ISP display to the concrete libdi-sensor thermometer
module and its acquisition endpoints. The app uses the thermister device and
separate TSU nodes, not an assumed generic OEM thermistor path.

The source also establishes adjustment IDs for mode/lower/upper thresholds,
signed 0/1/2 classification, and a consecutive-evaluation filter before publishing
a changed level. Actual adjustment values, physical units, polling cadence and
the final recording-stop/shutdown consumer remain unresolved. Generic OEM power
setters identify request node paths; they do not establish completed power-off
or persisted files.

The packaged kernel's named thermister miscdevice/read handler matches the
user-space device name and 40-byte request. The handler samples ADC channel 7,
maintains sample words/cached average and copies 40 bytes on its normal path.
Its CMOS sysfs show handler formats cached data. ADC words, cached average and
app-converted temperature are kept distinct; active boot selection remains
unknown.
